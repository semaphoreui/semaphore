┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ MVP LIMITATIONS                                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The MVP includes core functionality with some intentional limitations:

  • No circular workflows (loops not supported)
  • No advanced conditional expressions
  • Approval nodes auto-approve (manual approval not implemented)
  • Basic variable passing only
  • No fan-in synchronization
  • Polling-based updates (not WebSocket)

These limitations are documented and planned for future releases.
